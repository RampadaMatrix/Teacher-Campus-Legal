Privacy Policy

1. Our Privacy Promise

We believe in transparency and user control. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver educational updates, provide career tools, and ensure a smooth app experience. We do not sell or trade your personal data.

2. What Information We Collect

👤 Basic Account Information

If you choose to create a profile (for example, signing in with Google), we receive your basic profile name and email address to keep your bookmarks, notes, and study preferences saved across your sessions.

🔔 Notification Preferences

If you enable push notifications, your device uses a standard notification token to receive helpful alerts about recent exam announcements, syllabus releases, and recruitment news.

📱 Device & Diagnostic Insights

We receive non-sensitive technical data such as device model, OS version, and anonymous crash logs to ensure app stability, fix bugs, and optimize performance across screen sizes.

🧮 Calculator Inputs (Kept Locally)

When you use tools like the ROPA Salary Calculator or Utsashree Transfer Calculator, your inputs are processed entirely on your local device. We do not store or transmit your salary or financial calculations.

3. How We Use This Information

The information we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To maintain your account, bookmarks, and study preferences.
  • To send timely alerts regarding West Bengal educational notices and recruitment circulars.
  • To identify and resolve software errors and ensure app security.
  • To support the app through non-intrusive advertisements so our educational resources remain freely accessible.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power authentication, notifications, and advertising. These providers may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase (Authentication, Push Notifications & Crash Analytics) — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising & Mobile Ad Identifiers) — Ad Policy
  • Google Sign-In (Account Authentication) — Google Privacy Policy

5. You Are in Full Control of Your Data

  • In-App Account Deletion: You can delete your account and profile data at any time directly through the app settings (Profile > Delete Account).
  • Web-Based Deletion Request (Google Play Compliance): You can also submit an account and data deletion request via email to support@teachercampus.in with the subject line "Delete My Account" and your registered email address. Upon receiving your request, all associated personal records are permanently erased within 30 days.
  • Notification Controls: You can manage or disable push notifications anytime directly in your device's notification settings.

6. Security of Your Information

We employ standard, commercially reasonable safeguards to protect your personal data during transmission and storage. While no mobile transmission method is 100% secure, we continuously maintain safeguards to keep your information safe.

7. Children's Privacy (DPDP Act Compliance)

Teacher Campus is intended for adult educators, college trainees, and candidates preparing for teacher recruitment examinations. In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from individuals under 18 years of age.

8. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally refine this Privacy Policy to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be posted here with a revised date at the top of this page.
