3. Information We Collect

A. Personal Information You Provide

When you register or interact with the app, we may collect: name, email address, profile avatar (via Google OAuth / Appwrite authentication), bookmarked resources, and support communications.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

We automatically collect device specifications (model, OS version, resolution), anonymized IP address, app session statistics, and diagnostic crash logs to maintain app performance.

C. Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Data

We collect and store your device's FCM push token to transmit important notifications about exam circulars, syllabus updates, and result alerts.

D. Career Calculator Data (Local Only)

Data inputted into the ROPA 2019 Salary Calculator and Utsashree Transfer Calculator is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to remote servers.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide study resources, notes, and recruitment updates.
  • To authenticate accounts and sync bookmarks via Appwrite Cloud.
  • To dispatch timely push notifications regarding West Bengal education news.
  • To debug crashes, optimize performance, and prevent fraud.
  • To serve contextual in-app advertisements via Google AdMob.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliant)

You have the absolute right to delete your account and associated personal data at any time:

  • In-App: Navigate to More > Profile > Delete Account.
  • Web / Email Request: Email privacy@teachercampus.in or support@teachercampus.in with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email.

All personal data is permanently expunged within 30 days of receiving your request.

7. Security & Data Retention

We apply industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Personal data is retained only as long as your account remains active.

8. Children's Privacy

Teacher Campus is designed for adult educators and teacher recruitment aspirants (aged 18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13 years of age.
