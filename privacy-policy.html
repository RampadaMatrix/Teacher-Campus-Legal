Privacy & Data

Privacy Policy

Application: Teacher Campus • Last updated: August 2026
A quick note about Teacher Campus: Teacher Campus is an independent, non-governmental companion app designed to help educators, trainees, and aspirants across West Bengal with exam updates, study notes, and career tools. We are not an official government agency, and we aggregate public notices to make them easier to access.

1. Our Privacy Promise

We built Teacher Campus with a simple principle: your personal data belongs to you. We do not sell your personal information, and we only collect what is truly necessary to make your experience helpful, fast, and secure.

2. What Information We Collect (and Why)

To help you get the most out of the app, we may handle a few basic types of information:

👤 Basic Account Information

If you choose to create a profile (for example, signing in with Google), we receive your basic profile name and email address to keep your bookmarks and preferences saved across your sessions.

🔔 Notification Preferences

If you enable push notifications, your device uses a standard notification token to receive helpful alerts about recent exam announcements, syllabus releases, and news.

📱 Device & Performance Insights

We receive general, non-personal information such as your device model and app version. This helps us ensure the app looks great on your screen size, loads quickly, and stays free of bugs.

🧮 Calculator Inputs (Kept on your phone)

When you use the ROPA 2019 Salary Calculator or Utsashree Transfer Calculator, your entries are calculated directly on your device. We do not send your personal financial details or salary figures to any remote database.

3. How We Use This Information

Everything we collect is used solely to provide and improve your experience:

  • To keep your saved notes and bookmarked materials in sync.
  • To notify you about important educational updates and career news you care about.
  • To fix crashes, improve performance, and keep the app secure.
  • To show standard, non-intrusive advertisements that help keep Teacher Campus free for everyone.

4. Third-Party Services

Like most modern mobile apps, we partner with reliable services to support our features (such as sending push notifications, securing user logins, and displaying ads). These partners only process data to perform these specific tasks on our behalf and are bound to keep your data secure.

5. You Are in Full Control of Your Data

We believe in giving you complete control over your information:

  • Account & Data Deletion: You can delete your account and profile data anytime directly from the app's settings, or by reaching out to us. Once requested, your personal profile data is permanently removed.
  • Notification Controls: You can turn notifications on or off anytime directly in your phone's notification settings.

6. Keeping Your Information Safe

We use modern, industry-standard security measures to safeguard your information both while it travels to our servers and while stored. We regularly review our security practices to keep your data well protected.

7. Children's Privacy

Teacher Campus is designed for adult educators, college students, and teaching aspirants (aged 18 and older). We do not knowingly collect personal details from children under 13.

8. Updates to This Policy

As we add new helpful features to Teacher Campus, we may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. If we make meaningful changes, we will update the date at the top so you are always aware of how we protect your privacy.

9. We're Here to Help

If you ever have questions, suggestions, or need help managing your data, please reach out to us through the in-app support options or our application store page. We are always glad to assist you.
